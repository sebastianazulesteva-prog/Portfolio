Before you go inBeta

This was designed to be seen in a headset.

This view works, but the experience here is going to be worse — like watching an IMAX film on your phone, cropped vertically for TikTok.

Your phone is about to ask for motion & orientation access — please tap “Allow.” That permission is what lets you look around by physically moving your phone, which is the closest this gets to the real thing. It is used for nothing else: no data is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Decline and you can still look around by dragging.
